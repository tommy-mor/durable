Notes

  • rocksdb: blind Sum merges fold at compaction; reads slow under merge pressure.
  • redb: merges fold read-modify-write in the commit txn; reads stay flat.
  • sled: merges lower to put/delete against a commit overlay; reads stay flat.
  • SyncWal fsyncs per commit on rocksdb/redb; sled flush()es.
